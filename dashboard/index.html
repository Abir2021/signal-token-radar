MODEL NOTES

One signal, three inputs.

IMPLIED VALUETVL × 0.24 × growth × tenure
01 / SCALE

TVL sets the base

We start at 24% of snapshot TVL. This is a scenario anchor, not a claim about a future float or a market price.

02 / MOMENTUM

7-day change is bounded

Weekly TVL change enters an exponential multiplier with a −45% floor and +75% cap. One exceptional week cannot dominate the board.

03 / TENURE

Time adds context

Tracked age raises the maturity multiplier from 0.70 to 1.00 over three years. Missing dates use one year and are shown as unknown.

The snapshot contains no revenue, user counts, unlocks or token terms. The model cannot estimate probability or timing of issuance, and the watchlist itself can contain missing token records. Verify every protocol and risk before acting.
